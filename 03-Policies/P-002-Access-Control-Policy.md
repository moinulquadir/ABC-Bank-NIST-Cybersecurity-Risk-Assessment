# Access Control Policy

**Document ID:** P-002  
**Company:** Northstar Software Labs (fictional)  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** Information Security Manager  
**Approver:** Chief Operating Officer  
**Classification:** Internal  
**Status:** Approved for portfolio simulation  
**Review cycle:** At least annually and after material change

> **Portfolio note:** Northstar Software Labs is fictional. This document is an original implementation example aligned to ISO/IEC 27001:2022. It is not evidence of certification.

## 1. Purpose
Establish management direction and minimum security requirements for protecting company information, customer information, systems, services, and supporting business processes.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, temporary workers, systems, cloud services, offices, remote work environments, suppliers, and information within the Northstar ISMS scope. The in-scope service is the AWS-hosted B2B workflow SaaS platform and its supporting corporate and operational environment.

## 3. Policy Requirements
1. Access shall be based on business need, least privilege, and role requirements.
2. User identities shall be unique and shared accounts prohibited unless formally approved.
3. MFA shall be used for administrative, remote, and other risk-sensitive access.
4. Access shall be approved before provisioning and removed promptly when no longer required.
5. Privileged access shall receive enhanced controls and periodic review.
6. Access reviews shall be performed at least quarterly for critical systems.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities
- Executive management provides direction, resources, and risk acceptance authority.
- The Information Security Manager maintains the policy and coordinates the ISMS.
- System and asset owners implement controls appropriate to risk.
- Managers approve access and business exceptions within their authority.
- All personnel protect information and report suspected security events promptly.
- Internal Audit independently evaluates conformity and effectiveness.

## 5. Exceptions
Exceptions require documented business justification, risk assessment, compensating controls where practical, an owner, an expiry/review date, and approval by the designated risk authority.

## 6. Records and Evidence
Approvals, reviews, access records, security tickets, training records, audit results, risk decisions, and other supporting records are retained according to the records-retention requirements.

## 7. Enforcement
Non-compliance may result in corrective action, removal of access, contractual action, or disciplinary action, subject to applicable law and company procedures.

## 8. Review and Approval
The owner reviews this policy annually. Material changes to the ISMS, business, technology, legal obligations, or risk profile trigger an earlier review.
