# Security Awareness Policy

**Document ID:** P-019  
**Company:** Northstar Software Labs (fictional)  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** Information Security Manager  
**Approver:** Chief Operating Officer  
**Classification:** Internal  
**Status:** Approved for portfolio simulation  
**Review cycle:** Annual or after material change

> This is an original portfolio implementation example aligned to ISO/IEC 27001:2022. Northstar is fictional and is not represented as ISO certified.

## 1. Purpose
Define management requirements for security awareness within the Northstar ISMS.

## 2. Scope
Applies to relevant personnel, information, applications, infrastructure, cloud services, suppliers, and business processes within the ISMS scope.

## 3. Requirements
1. All personnel shall complete security awareness training at onboarding and periodically thereafter.
2. Role-specific training shall be provided where responsibilities create additional risk.
3. Training completion shall be recorded.
4. Security awareness exercises may include phishing simulations and scenario-based learning.
5. Overdue training shall be followed up by management.

## 4. Responsibilities
- **Policy owner:** maintains requirements, monitors implementation, and coordinates review.
- **Asset/process owners:** implement controls and retain evidence.
- **Managers:** enforce requirements and approve business decisions within authority.
- **Employees and contractors:** comply and report concerns.
- **Internal Audit:** independently assesses conformity and effectiveness.

## 5. Exceptions
Exceptions require documented rationale, affected asset/process, risk assessment, compensating controls where appropriate, owner, approval, and expiry/review date.

## 6. Records
Relevant approvals, tickets, logs, registers, test results, training records, audit findings, and corrective actions are retained according to the records-retention requirements.

## 7. Review
The policy owner reviews this document at least annually and after material organizational, technology, contractual, legal, or risk changes.
