# PR-024 — ISMS Management Review Procedure

**Company:** Northstar Software Labs (fictional)  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** Process Owner  
**Approver:** Information Security Manager  
**Classification:** Internal  
**Status:** Controlled portfolio document

> Original implementation example. All company data and evidence are simulated.

## 1. Purpose
Provide a repeatable, auditable method for the process within the ISMS.

## 2. Procedure
1. Prepare inputs covering risks, objectives, audit results, incidents, performance, changes, and improvement opportunities\n2. conduct management review\n2. record decisions, actions, owners, and due dates\n2. track closure.

## 3. Required Records
Retain approvals, actions, reviewers, dates, outcomes, and linked evidence.

## 4. Escalation
Escalate material control failures, overdue high-risk actions, and unresolved exceptions to management.

## 5. Review
Review annually and after material changes.
