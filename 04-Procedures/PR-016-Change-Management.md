## 1. Purpose
Provide a repeatable, auditable method for performing this process within the ISMS.

## 2. Trigger and Scope
This procedure applies whenever the process is initiated for an in-scope system, employee, supplier, security event, change, or control activity.

## 3. Procedure
1. Create a change request with reason, scope, risk, testing, and rollback plan.
2. Obtain required approval before implementation.
3. Perform testing appropriate to change risk.
4. Implement during the approved window.
5. Validate service and security controls.
6. Close the change with implementation evidence.

## 4. Required Records
At minimum retain the applicable request/ticket, approval, action evidence, reviewer, date, outcome, and linked corrective action or exception.

## 5. Escalation
Escalate missed deadlines, material security impact, unresolved high-risk findings, suspected incidents, or control failures to the responsible manager and Information Security Manager.

## 6. Metrics
Typical measures include completion rate, overdue actions, time to complete, exceptions, repeat findings, and effectiveness of corrective actions.

## 7. Review
Review annually and after significant process, system, legal, contractual, or risk changes.
