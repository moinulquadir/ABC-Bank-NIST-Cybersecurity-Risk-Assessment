# 13 — Management Review

## Review purpose
Top management reviews the ISMS at planned intervals to ensure continuing suitability, adequacy and effectiveness.

## Inputs reviewed
- Status of actions from previous review
- Changes in internal/external issues
- Interested-party/customer requirements
- Information-security performance and metrics
- Risk register and residual-risk position
- Internal audit results
- CAPA status
- Security incidents
- Training and awareness
- Supplier performance
- Resource requirements
- Opportunities for continual improvement

## Example management dashboard
| Metric | Target | Result | Decision |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| MFA coverage | 100% | 100% | Maintain |
| Critical vulnerabilities past SLA | 0 | 1 | Escalate |
| Security training completion | >=98% | 99% | Maintain |
| Backup restore tests passed | 100% | 100% | Maintain |
| High risks with owners | 100% | 100% | Maintain |
| CAPA overdue | 0 | 0 | Maintain |
| Supplier reviews overdue | 0 | 0 | Maintain |

## Management decisions
1. Continue the ISMS and proceed to certification-readiness assessment.
2. Fund additional cloud-security engineering capacity.
3. Require critical vulnerability exceptions to receive executive approval.
4. Maintain annual internal audit and management review cycle.
5. Add supplier reassessment automation to the continual-improvement backlog.

## Outputs
- Resource decisions approved.
- Risk acceptance confirmed for remaining medium residual risks.
- CAPA closure accepted.
- Continual-improvement actions assigned.
- Certification-readiness assessment authorized.
