# 02 — ISMS Scope and Context

## Clause 4 — Context of the organization

### Internal issues
- Rapid SaaS product releases
- Hybrid/remote workforce
- Cloud-first architecture
- Dependence on third-party SaaS
- Engineering access to production systems
- Customer contractual security requirements
- Growing security/compliance workload

### External issues
- Cyber threats against SaaS providers
- Privacy and contractual obligations
- Customer due-diligence requirements
- Cloud and software supply-chain concentration
- Regulatory expectations in customer markets
- Increasing ransomware and credential-theft risk

### Interested parties
| Party | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Customers | Confidentiality, availability, secure development |
| Employees | Clear responsibilities and safe working practices |
| Regulators | Applicable legal/regulatory compliance |
| Suppliers | Security requirements and incident notification |
| Executives | Risk visibility and business resilience |
| Certification body | Conformity evidence |

## ISMS scope statement
The ISMS covers the people, processes, information, applications, infrastructure and third parties used to design, develop, deploy, support and operate the Northstar Workflow SaaS platform, including corporate IT services supporting those activities.

**Included:** production AWS environment, development/test environments, source-code repositories, CI/CD, identity services, endpoint devices used for in-scope work, security monitoring, customer support systems, HR processes supporting in-scope personnel, vendor management, incident response and business continuity.

**Excluded from direct operational control:** AWS data-center physical infrastructure and other cloud-provider facilities controlled by the provider. Relevant supplier and shared-responsibility controls remain in scope through contractual assurance, due diligence and monitoring.

## ISMS boundaries
- Business: Northstar Software Labs
- Technology: AWS production/non-production, GitHub, CI/CD, endpoint fleet, SaaS business systems
- People: employees and relevant contractors
- Geography: New York HQ and remote workers
- Interfaces: customers, suppliers, cloud provider, payment/identity providers

## Clause 5 — Leadership
Top management approves the information-security policy, assigns responsibilities, provides resources and reviews ISMS performance. The Head of Information Security has authority to coordinate the ISMS and report material risks.

## Information Security Policy — commitment
Northstar commits to protecting information through risk-based controls that preserve confidentiality, integrity and availability; meeting applicable obligations; continually improving the ISMS; and assigning clear accountability for information security.
