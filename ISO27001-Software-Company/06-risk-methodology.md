# 06 — Risk Methodology

## Scoring
Likelihood and impact are each scored 1–5.

### Likelihood
1 Rare | 2 Unlikely | 3 Possible | 4 Likely | 5 Almost certain

### Impact
1 Insignificant | 2 Minor | 3 Moderate | 4 Major | 5 Severe

**Risk score = Likelihood × Impact**

| Score | Rating | Treatment expectation |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | Low | Monitor or accept |
| 5–9 | Medium | Treat or formally accept |
| 10–16 | High | Treatment plan required |
| 17–25 | Critical | Immediate executive attention |

## Assessment workflow
1. Identify asset/process.
2. Identify threat event.
3. Identify vulnerability/control weakness.
4. Score inherent likelihood and impact.
5. Identify existing controls.
6. Select treatment: modify, avoid, share or retain.
7. Define treatment actions.
8. Assign risk owner.
9. Estimate residual risk.
10. Obtain risk acceptance where required.
11. Monitor and reassess.

## Risk acceptance
High and critical residual risks require documented acceptance by the accountable business owner and review by the ISMS owner. Critical risks require executive escalation.

## Risk assumptions
Scores are qualitative and designed for portfolio demonstration. A real organization would calibrate scales using historical incidents, business impact analysis, threat intelligence and regulatory/customer requirements.
