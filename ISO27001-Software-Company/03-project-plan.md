## Change control
Changes affecting ISMS scope, risk acceptance, control design or major suppliers require documented impact analysis and approval by the ISMS owner. Emergency changes are documented retrospectively within one business day.

## Project acceptance
The project is accepted when the closure criteria in `14-project-closure.md` are met and management has formally accepted the residual-risk position.
