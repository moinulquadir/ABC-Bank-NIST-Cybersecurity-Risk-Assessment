# 01 — Project Initiation

## 1. Business case
Northstar's customer base increasingly requested evidence of a mature information-security program during procurement. Security practices existed, but they were distributed across engineering, IT, HR, legal and operations. Management approved an ISO/IEC 27001 implementation project to create one governed ISMS and reduce inconsistent risk decisions.

## 2. Project charter
**Project:** Northstar ISMS Implementation  
**Sponsor:** Chief Operating Officer  
**Project Manager:** Information Security Program Manager  
**ISMS Owner:** Head of Information Security  
**Target duration:** 9 months  
**Target outcome:** Certification-ready ISMS, subject to an independent certification body's audit.

### Objectives
- Establish an ISMS aligned with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 clauses 4–10.
- Define and approve the ISMS scope.
- Establish a repeatable information-security risk process.
- Complete a 93-control Annex A applicability assessment.
- Implement prioritized controls and collect operating evidence.
- Complete an internal audit and management review.
- Close material nonconformities before certification.
- Create a continual-improvement backlog after project closure.

### Success criteria
- ISMS scope and policy approved.
- Risk register reviewed and accepted by risk owners.
- SoA approved and traceable to risks/requirements.
- Priority controls implemented with evidence.
- Internal audit completed.
- CAPA items have owners and due dates.
- Management review completed.
- Certification-readiness decision documented.

## 5. Project governance
Weekly project working group; biweekly steering review; monthly executive reporting. Major risk acceptance requires the relevant risk owner and ISMS owner. Material exceptions are escalated to the COO.
